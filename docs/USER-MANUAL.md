# OPT Plus Digital - User Documentation

## Introduction

The OPT Plus Digital System is a mobile-first web application designed to streamline the Operation Timbang Plus process in the Philippines. It enables Barangay Health Workers (BHWs) and Barangay Nutrition Scholars (BNS) to collect, manage, and analyze child growth data efficiently.

## Core Workflows

### 1. Child Registration

- Navigate to the **Children** section and click **Add Child**.
- Fill in the required information: Family Serial Number, Name, Sex, Date of Birth, and Barangay.
- The system will automatically calculate the child's age and check for potential duplicates.

### 2. Recording Measurements

- Select a child from the list to view their profile.
- Click **Add Measurement**.
- Enter the **Weight (kg)** and **Height/Length (cm)**.
- The system instantly calculates the WHO Z-scores (WFA, HFA, WFH) and determines the nutritional status (Normal, MAM, SAM, etc.).

### 3. Offline Usage (PWA)

- The application works even without an internet connection.
- Ensure you have "installed" the app to your home screen (Add to Home Screen).
- Open the app while online once to cache necessary data.
- When offline, you can still register measurements. These will be saved locally and automatically synced when you regain internet connectivity.

### 4. Reports & Analytics

- **BNS users** can access the **Reports** section.
- Generate **OPT Plus Summary Reports**, **Master Lists**, and **Target Lists**.
- Export any report to **PDF** for printing or **Excel** (eOPT compatible) for official submission.

## Nutritional Status Classifications

- **Normal:** Child is within healthy growth ranges.
- **MAM (Moderate Acute Malnutrition):** Child requires nutritional supplement and monitoring.
- **SAM (Severe Acute Malnutrition):** Child requires immediate medical intervention.
- **Overweight/Obese:** Child's weight is high for their height/age.

## Support

For technical issues, contact your Barangay Health Center or the system administrator.
